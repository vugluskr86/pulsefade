# PULSEFADE Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

PULSEFADE (the "Game") uses the GamePush platform for publishing, cloud saves, leaderboards, and advertising. This policy describes what data is collected and how it is used.

## Data Collection

### GamePush SDK

- **Player ID** — automatically assigned by GamePush for progress sync and leaderboards.
- **Game progress** — score, records, in-game currency (pulses), unlocked cosmetics, Journey progress, mission progress. This data is saved to GamePush cloud storage.
- **Analytics** — aggregated gameplay events (round start, finish, ad view, leaderboard open). Used to improve game balance and content.

### Advertising

- The Game shows rewarded video and fullscreen ads through ad networks connected via GamePush.
- Ad networks may collect device identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Usage

Data is used exclusively for:

- Saving and syncing game progress across devices.
- Displaying leaderboards.
- Serving advertisements.
- Analyzing player behavior to improve the game.

## Third-Party Data Sharing

Data is shared with:

- GamePush (publishing platform) — for progress storage and ad delivery.
- Ad networks — only through GamePush, without direct game access to ad SDKs.
- We do not sell player data or share it with other third parties.

## Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. The Game does not request or store personal information (name, email, phone, photos).

## Player Rights

You may request deletion of your game data by contacting support at `support@pulsefade.example.com`. Data can also be cleared locally by deleting browser cookies and localStorage.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on the game's store listing page.
